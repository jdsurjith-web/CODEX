# Demo Flow

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Explain the industrial waste valorization problem.
3. Navigate to Analyze Waste.
4. Enter Foundry Sand and a weekly quantity of 2.5 tonnes.
5. Run the AI valorization analysis.
6. Show the loading pipeline and results.
7. Explain the 91/100 recommendation and why the pathway is selected.
8. Open Pathway Intelligence and compare reuse, recycling, recovery, and disposal.
9. Adjust contamination in the What-If simulator to show the recommendation changing.
10. Open Market Matching and explain the prototype users.
11. Open Forecast to explain future planning.

This flow demonstrates the complete decision-support story without claiming real-world validation.
